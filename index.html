Privacy Policy

App: AquaBuddy – Water Reminder
Effective date: 20 November 2026
Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AquaBuddy – Water Reminder (“AquaBuddy”, “the app”, “we”, “us” or “our”), developed by Codingja Technologies, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored by the app

AquaBuddy is designed to store your hydration information locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Water-intake entries and related dates or times;
  • Your daily hydration goal;
  • Your preferred measurement unit (ml or oz);
  • Your reminder preferences and app settings; and
  • History and statistics calculated from the information you enter.

We do not operate an AquaBuddy account system or our own cloud server for this information. Based on the app's current configuration, this information is stored on your device and is not sent to Codingja Technologies.

2. How information is used

The locally stored information is used only to provide the app's features, such as recording water intake, calculating progress toward your daily goal, showing history and statistics, remembering your preferences, and scheduling reminders when you enable them.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission where required by your Android version, AquaBuddy may schedule local notifications on your device. Reminder scheduling is used to remind you to drink water. The app does not send your hydration records to a remote notification server as part of this local reminder feature.

4. Data sharing and third-party services

Based on the app configuration described by the developer, AquaBuddy does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud services that collect or receive your hydration information. Codingja Technologies does not sell, rent, or share your hydration records with third parties.

Your Android device manufacturer or operating system may provide device-level backup, restore, or notification services. Those services are governed by the relevant provider's terms and privacy settings. Device backup behaviour may depend on your Android settings and the app's backup configuration.

5. Data storage, security and retention

Your AquaBuddy information is stored locally within the app's storage area on your device. We do not maintain a copy on our own servers. We use the app's local storage mechanisms and rely on Android's device-level security protections. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your locally stored information remains on the device until it is deleted using available in-app controls, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. If device-level backup is enabled, backup copies may be handled according to your Android or backup-provider settings.

6. Permissions

AquaBuddy may request notification permission to deliver reminders. The app should request only permissions needed for the features you choose to use. It does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location to provide its core water-tracking features.

7. Children's privacy

AquaBuddy is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app stores the information you enter locally and does not send it to our servers, we do not knowingly receive children's personal information through the app. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of mobile apps.

8. Your choices

You can stop using AquaBuddy at any time. You can manage notification permission through your Android device settings. You can remove locally stored app information using available in-app controls, by clearing AquaBuddy's app storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove locally stored records.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
